# Financial modeling source review

Reviewed November 3, 2026. Scope covers investment-oriented three-statement, operating, DCF, acquisition and LBO models in Excel. The review prioritizes original standard setters, official data providers, academic teaching by the author and documentation from tool maintainers. The sources below inform the skill's methods. Public access and reuse rights vary, and open-source software is identified separately. The SFG workbook provides the visual reference. These sources provide no basis for claiming formal FAST, accounting or regulatory compliance for a teaching workbook.

## Modeling standards and source data

| Source reviewed | Practice adopted and scope |
| --- | --- |
| [ICAEW Financial Modelling Code](https://www.icaew.com/-/media/corporate/files/technical/technology/excel/financial-modelling-code.ashx) and [Twenty principles, 2024 edition](https://www.icaew.com/technical/technology/excel-community/20-principles-for-good-spreadsheet-practice-2024-edition) | Define audience, question, controls and materiality before building. Centralize inputs, keep formula paths readable, use consistent periods, build checks from the start and arrange independent review. Keep operating instructions and review history in the delivery notes where the workbook style calls for clean sheets. |
| [FAST Standard, 02c](https://www.fast-standard.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/FAST-Standard-02c-July-2019.pdf) | Apply consistent time axes, clear calculation blocks and short formulas. Separate inputs, work and presentation. Use helper rows for different economic steps. Preserve the supplied style when a visual prescription differs from SFG. |
| [SEC EDGAR APIs](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-application-programming-interfaces) and [Arelle](https://github.com/Arelle/Arelle) | Use SEC submissions and standardized company facts as discovery inputs. Select facts using accession, filing date, concept, units, dates and period length. Resolve issuer-specific tags and dimensional disclosures against the filed statements or use Arelle to parse and validate the filing instance. Retain an as-filed snapshot for backtests and distinguish restatements. Tie mapped historical values to filed statements. A parsed XBRL fact still requires economic classification. |
| [SEC non-GAAP interpretations](https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/staff-guidance/corporation-finance-interpretations/non-gaap-financial-measures) | Preserve reported results and show issuer adjustments separately from analyst adjustments. Track recurring cash costs, gains as well as charges, SBC, and definition changes. For comparisons, match enterprise value and EBITDA adjustments across both sides of the multiple. The C&DIs govern relevant issuer disclosures and serve as a discipline reference for internal underwriting. |
| [New York Fed reference rates](https://www.newyorkfed.org/markets/reference-rates), [ARRC SOFR conventions](https://www.newyorkfed.org/arrc/sofr-transition), [ALFRED vintage help](https://alfred.stlouisfed.org/help) and [BLS ECI](https://www.bls.gov/eci/home.htm) | Record rate basis and as-of date. Overnight SOFR, a backward-looking compounded rate and a forward term rate are distinct inputs. Apply the contractual tenor, spread, floor and convention when forecasting debt. Use vintage data for historical backtests and match a wage or compensation index to the cost being escalated. |

## Accounting, transactions and valuation

| Source reviewed | Practice adopted and scope |
| --- | --- |
| [IAS 7 cash flows](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ias-7-statement-of-cash-flows/) and [IFRS 18 presentation](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ifrs-18-presentation-and-disclosure-in-financial-statements/) | Keep distinct cash and financing-liability roll-forwards. Separate actual cash flows from noncash changes in debt and consolidation. Confirm reporting basis and adoption date before mapping captions. IFRS 18 applies to annual periods beginning on or after January 1, 2027, with early application permitted. |
| [IFRS 3 business combinations](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ifrs-3-business-combinations/) and [FASB updates to the Codification](https://www.fasb.org/standards/accounting-standard-updates) | Align acquisition date, consideration, acquired net assets and goodwill. Separate cash paid, debt assumed, new financing, fees, contingent consideration, acquired working capital and subsequent amortization. Verify applicable GAAP and close dates. For a simplified synthetic schedule, explicitly scope any omitted items and use zero balances only where the invented deal terms support them. |
| [IFRS 16 leases](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ifrs-16-leases/) and [FASB Topic 842](https://fasb.org/projects/current-projects/leases-398331) | Maintain separate accounting and valuation views where leases are material. Match the treatment of lease cash flows, operating earnings, leverage and the EV-to-equity bridge. For sale-leaseback, identify sale qualification, proceeds, any recognized gain, ongoing cash rent, lease obligations and differences between accounting and economic ownership. |
| [Damodaran introduction to valuation](https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/New_Home_Page/background/valintro.htm), [valuation lectures](https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/New_Home_Page/lectures/val.html) and [terminal value materials](https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/New_Home_Page/Inv2ed.htm) | Match cash flow and discount rate. Unlevered cash flow excludes financing flows. Use a consistent currency, nominal or real basis, market-value weights and discount date. For a perpetuity, require WACC > g and forecast a normalized year N+1 that funds its growth. Link steady-state reinvestment to growth and incremental ROIC. Cross-check the terminal value with an implied multiple, and report the portion of enterprise value attributable to terminal value. |
| [Damodaran free cash flow notes](https://people.stern.nyu.edu/adamodar/pdfiles/blog/FreeCF.pdf) | Acquired growth requires acquisition reinvestment. Separate purchased revenue and EBITDA from organic growth, and count deal cash, debt, equity consideration and dilution exactly once in the relevant views. Normalize a lumpy acquisition program explicitly when it is embedded in projected growth. |

## Spreadsheet tooling and verification

| Tool or reference | Appropriate use and release test |
| --- | --- |
| [openpyxl](https://openpyxl.readthedocs.io/en/stable/tutorial.html) | Open-source XLSX inspection and supported edits. Read formula text and last stored results in separate passes. A cached result reflects the last calculation engine to save it. Keep read-only access for private reference workbooks. Test retention of relevant objects before editing a feature-rich workbook because some elements, such as shapes, can be lost on a round trip. |
| [XlsxWriter formula support](https://xlsxwriter.readthedocs.io/working_with_formulas.html) and [FAQ](https://xlsxwriter.readthedocs.io/faq.html) | Open-source authoring for new files. A formula record, a cached value and a recalc flag are separate. Default cached zeros provide no evidence of successful calculation. Independently computed caches can support previews, with a target-engine recalculation still needed to verify formula behavior. |
| [LibreOffice startup parameters](https://help.libreoffice.org/latest/en-US/text/shared/guide/start_parameters.html) | Open-source batch alternative for compatible workbooks. Use an isolated profile and a separate output directory. Compare reopened formulas, values and relevant presentation features before accepting rewritten files. Qualify what was verified in Calc and confirm engine-sensitive features in the intended client. |
| [Microsoft Excel calculation guidance](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/office/vba/excel/concepts/excel-performance/excel-improving-calculation-performance) and [calculation mode](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/troubleshoot/microsoft-365-apps/excel/current-mode-of-calculation) | Excel is the target runtime for an Excel-first release. Run a full dependency rebuild and recalc in an isolated session, save, reopen, and verify formula caches. Use a named engine version in the release evidence where available. Save the base case and check that workbook calculation mode supports expected updates on edit. |
| [Microsoft What-If Data Tables](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/excel/calculate-multiple-results-by-using-a-data-table) and [Excel recalculation](https://learn.microsoft.com/office/client-developer/excel/excel-recalculation) | A one-variable table can report multiple outputs, and a two-variable table uses one output formula. Confirm input cells, calculate mode and a fresh grid in the target engine. A simple explicit grid is preferable for portable scenarios where every case needs to update deterministically. |
| [Microsoft financial functions](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/excel/financial-functions-reference), [XIRR behavior](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dotnet/api/microsoft.office.interop.excel.worksheetfunction.xirr?view=excel-pia) and [XNPV](https://support.microsoft.com/en-gb/excel/functions/xnpv-function) | Use real cash-flow dates with both positive and negative cash flows. Excel solves XIRR iteratively using a 365-day basis. Independently evaluate dated NPV at the reported root, check MOIC from gross cash flows and investigate multiple sign reversals before relying on an IRR. |
| [Formualizer](https://github.com/psu3d0/formualizer) | Candidate open-source supplementary formula engine. Its maintainers document supported functions and cache writing. Establish parity on actual workbook features and golden cases before relying on any alternative engine for release. The project was reviewed for capabilities and was outside the benchmark scope. |

## v0.6 gap audit

Baseline artifact fingerprint (SHA-256): 075977e14cc1d5b4b566345830e910205371f762da5f645ee83d732bd7528880. The archive is held separately from this repository.

The baseline already has a coherent SFG architecture, a case bank, three-statement build order, BASE roll-forwards, fixed and variable cost drivers, market-weighted WACC, a debt waterfall and a two-path audit requirement. The gaps below turn those practices into specific release criteria. These are project choices informed by the sources above.

| Priority | Baseline gap | Skill refinement and acceptance evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Release | Historical normalization is described, with limited controls for units, fiscal periods, reclassifications and revisions. | Capture as-of date, period start/end, unit, currency, sign and source locator. Check the source-to-model mapping, quarterly versus YTD inputs, historical statement parity and reported-to-adjusted bridge. |
| Release | Cumulative FCF is expected to equal the change in net debt under a broad definition. | Use a signed net-debt bridge with cash movement, borrowing/repayments, M&A, distributions, equity proceeds, PIK, FX, assumed debt and other noncash effects as relevant. Keep cash and debt roll-forwards separate. Reconcile every period. |
| Release | Revolver cash plug and average-balance interest can conceal liquidity failure or create iterative dependencies. | Calculate draws after testing contractual capacity. Show any uncovered shortfall. Use beginning balances or time-weight known flows in the default model. For intentional iteration, show convergence and validate limiting cases independently. Test sufficient and exhausted revolver headroom. |
| Release | A self-referencing case snapshot can appear current after underlying inputs change. | Calculate live scenarios through explicit case references or verified native tables. Label a manual snapshot with its case, valuation date and model version. Check center-cell parity and perturb an upstream input to prove propagation. |
| Release | DCF gives the basic equations with few terminal and enterprise-equity consistency guards. | Explicitly model next-year steady-state cash flow, growth-funded reinvestment, WACC > g, terminal EV share and the bridge with matching leases and other debt-like items. Reproduce base EV and equity independently and exercise invalid WACC/g cells. |
| Release | XIRR, MOIC and reverse underwriting lack date and root acceptance criteria. | Check dates, cash signs, terminal proceeds, dated NPV at XIRR and gross cash MOIC. For reverse DCF or target-IRR entry, state the solved variable, bracket the solution and run the result forward to recover the target. |
| High | Acquisition layering separates bought growth with little detail on close or post-close balances. | Schedule close dates, partial-year ownership, financing, net assets and consideration. Distinguish cash investment, accounting movement and run-rate results. Check toggling M&A off zeros acquisition flows, goodwill additions and acquired earnings while organic drivers remain active. |
| High | A parity row and formula scan alone can miss mutually wrong links, stale caches or numeric literals with different purposes. | Compare independent recomputations, distinguish structural constants from economic assumptions, validate all cached formula results, inspect links and use declared tolerances by unit. Verify no merges and no links to reference workbooks in a synthetic release. |
| High | Stress testing is stated generally. | Recompute base, downside, upside, liquidity and investment levers. Use controlled one-driver changes and expected direction where the model's economics establish it. Verify exact step values, scenario-to-output wiring and a base case restored after testing. |
| Scope-dependent | Straight-line tax, flat other balances and generic comp screens can be weak under stress or for specialized sectors. | Escalate to explicit NOL, deferred tax, lease, equity dilution and project-level working capital schedules when material. Use segment, size, geography, timing and accounting basis to select comparables. Scope specialized valuation models such as regulated lenders and insurers separately. |

The review incorporates conclusions in original language. Source material, training decks, downloaded models and actual company data are outside the distribution bundle.
